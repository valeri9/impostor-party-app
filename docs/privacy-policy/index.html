Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Impostor Party is built to work entirely on your device. Here's exactly what that means.

What We Collect

Nothing. The app makes no network calls, has no analytics, no ads, and no account or sign-in of any kind.

Where Your Data Lives

Player names, your chosen mode and settings, your skin and language choices, and which skins you own are all stored only in local storage on your device. None of it is ever transmitted anywhere or shared with anyone.

Permissions

The app requests vibration access only to give button presses haptic feedback during play — nothing else.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic skins are purchased through Google Play's own billing system. That transaction — including your payment details — is handled entirely by Google under its own privacy policy. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account, not this device, so a skin you've bought appears automatically on any phone signed into that account. Impostor Party never sees or stores your payment information — only, locally on your device, which skins you own.
